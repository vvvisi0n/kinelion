The Evaluation Process

Four steps. No pressure.

We designed our clinical evaluation program around the way your team actually works. Simple, structured, and entirely on your schedule.

01
Request Units

Submit your request below. We review your facility profile and confirm your evaluation package within 48 hours.

02
Receive and Orient

Evaluation units ship directly to your facility. Our clinical team provides a virtual orientation session for your OT or PT staff.

03
Patient Testing

Your team uses Kinelion products with a defined patient group over a 30 day evaluation period. We provide structured feedback tools to document outcomes.

04
Decision and Partnership

At the end of the evaluation period you decide. If Kinelion is the right fit, we move to an institutional partnership. If not, return the units with no obligation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is there a cost for the evaluation program?

No. Kinelion evaluation units are provided at no cost for the 30 day evaluation period. We cover shipping both ways. There is no financial commitment until you choose to partner with us.

Which products are included in the evaluation?

Evaluation packages include the Kinelion One base unit and your choice of two activity modules. We recommend the Everyday Module and one specialist module based on your patient population.

What patient population is Kinelion designed for?

Kinelion products are designed for anyone with limited hand dexterity. This includes tetraplegic and spinal cord injury patients, stroke survivors, individuals with progressive neurological conditions, and elderly patients with reduced grip strength.

How does the OT orientation work?

We provide a 45 minute virtual orientation session with your OT or PT team before evaluation units are deployed. This covers product application, module swapping, wrist angle configuration, and documentation protocols.

What happens after the evaluation period?

You receive a full evaluation summary from our clinical team and you make your decision. Facilities that choose to partner with Kinelion receive preferred institutional pricing and are recognized as founding clinical partners.

Become a founding clinical partner.

We are selecting a small group of founding clinical partners to co-develop and validate Kinelion One. If you want to help shape the future of adaptive equipment, we want to hear from you.
