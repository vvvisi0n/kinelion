Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from clinicians.

Is there any cost to join the founding clinical circle?

No. There is no cost and no commitment. We are asking for your clinical expertise and insights in exchange for first access and preferred pricing when Kinelion One launches.

What does Kinelion One do?

Kinelion One is a forearm-mounted adaptive platform with a universal magnetic docking system and interchangeable activity modules. It is designed for tetraplegics and anyone with limited hand dexterity. Full specifications are available upon request.

What patient population is Kinelion designed for?

Kinelion products are designed for anyone with limited hand dexterity. This includes tetraplegic and spinal cord injury patients, stroke survivors, individuals with progressive neurological conditions, and elderly patients with reduced grip strength.

How much time does this require from my team?

Minimal. An initial 30 minute conversation with our team is all we ask. From there we work around your schedule and communicate on your terms.

When will Kinelion One be available?

Kinelion One is currently in development. Founding clinical partners will be the first to know when units are ready and will receive preferred access and pricing before the general market launch.

Become a founding clinical partner.

We are selecting a small group of founding clinical partners to co-develop and validate Kinelion One. If you want to help shape the future of adaptive equipment, we want to hear from you.
