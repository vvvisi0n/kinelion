The Platform

Not a device. A universal interface.

The Kinelion One is not adaptive equipment. It is a performance platform. A forearm-mounted system that creates a universal interface between the user's arm and the world around them.

Every activity demands something different. The gym requires grip and load capacity. The kitchen requires precision and angle control. Daily life requires flexibility and speed. The Kinelion One adapts to all of them through a single base unit and a growing ecosystem of interchangeable modules.

Designed with occupational therapy specialists. Built by a founder who relies on adaptive equipment every single day.

Engineering

Three zones. One unified system.

Each zone serves a specific biomechanical purpose. Together they distribute load, stabilize position, and create the foundation for every module in the ecosystem.

🦾
Zone 01
Forearm Anchor

Primary structural support with even pressure distribution across the forearm. Magnetic closure system designed for one-handed application. No pressure points. No exposed hardware.

⚙️
Zone 02
Wrist Stabilizer

Adjustable angle positioning at 15, 30, and 45 degrees. User sets their preferred angle by activity. The device holds that position precisely throughout use. Hidden ratchet mechanism.

🔌
Zone 03
Adaptive Palm Dock

The universal mounting platform. A magnetic locking system that accepts all Kinelion activity modules. Audible click confirmation. Tactile feedback. Any module swaps in seconds.
