The Kinelion Story

Built from the inside out.

Victor Landing has lived with tetraplegia for over a decade. Before his diagnosis he founded a private personal training practice where he worked with executives, artists, and professionals through a philosophy of functional programming: addressing what the body truly needs, not just what the client asks for.

When the adaptive equipment market failed to keep pace with his active lifestyle, he built his own solutions. A wrist strap with a hook. A cable attachment for the gym. Each prototype broke. Each one got rebuilt stronger. That process became the foundation of Kinelion.

"Founded by a tetraplegic athlete who got tired of waiting for the right adaptive equipment to exist, so he built it himself."

Kinelion is the company the adaptive equipment industry has needed for decades. A premium performance brand built by someone who uses adaptive equipment every single day, designed with occupational therapy specialists, and grounded in one belief: independence is not a luxury. It is a right.
